# Book Journal roadmap

Rule for everything that adds to the Book Log: it gets an on/off switch in Settings (a "Features" tab), off or on as you choose, so anything you don't use stays out of the way. Turning a feature off hides it; it never deletes the data.

## To do, in the order I'd suggest
1. **Feature switches** (Settings > Features): the foundation for the items marked (switch).
2. **Quotes and favorite passages** (switch): per read, with page or timestamp and an optional note; a random quote on Home.
3. **Mood and pace tags** (switch): two new pick-lists, managed in Settings > Lists.
4. **Content warnings** (switch): its own pick-list.
5. **Why I stopped, for DNF reads** (switch): a short reason and the page or time where you stopped.
6. **Where the recommendation came from, on the TBR** (switch): who or what recommended it, and the date added.
7. **Owned vs. wanted, per format**: mark whether you own a physical, ebook or audio copy; the Shelf and TBR views separate "own it" from "want it, don't have a copy". No price.
8. **Reading heatmap**: a year grid of the days you read.
9. **Reading speed**: pages or hours per day per book, from start and finish dates.
10. **Rating compared with source, format, genre and mood**, plus favorite authors by rating.
11. **Year in Review** (like Spotify Wrapped): a scrollable summary page.
12. **Customize the Book Log** (low priority): add cards you define, resize, rearrange; applies to the whole app first, with room for templates by format or per book later. New card types can store new data, and that data can become goal metrics.
13. **Reading timer** (very low priority): explore how it could work.
14. **Sync and a phone companion** (low priority, wanted later).

## Not now (keep in mind)
- Library due dates and holds
- ISBN and metadata lookup
- Duplicate and series-gap finder
- Goodreads and StoryGraph import
